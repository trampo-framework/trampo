# runbooks

What a team knows about its systems, as system runbooks and flow runbooks with an index. Loaded from code on day one
as drafts, kept true by every session that learns something, and verified by replay once the team has a test
environment. Needs only the core.

The team owns `modes/runbooks.md` and `runbooks/README.md` once they are copied, and edits them directly: they win
over the skills.
